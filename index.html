About Cherry Collins

Cherry Collins is a London-based luxury brand and marketing leader. She is currently Global Strategy Partner at Havas Media Lux, where she acts as strategic consultant to luxury brands including Hermès, the LVMH Maisons, Dolce & Gabbana, Emirates, Laura Mercier and Abercrombie & Kent Travel Group, shaping their annual communications and media strategy. Her work spans fashion, beauty, skincare, fragrance, wellness, jewellery, watches, travel and hospitality.

Her experience spans luxury retail and e-commerce at Net-a-Porter and Selfridges, editorial at Grazia, and full P&L ownership as employee no. 6 at a fashion-tech start-up. That mix means she thinks like a brand owner, a publisher and a media planner at once.

She works in a deeply human way, using AI as a sparring partner to sharpen research and widen the thinking, while taste, judgement and relationships stay firmly hers.
